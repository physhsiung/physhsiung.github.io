Cosmological Bootstrap

Advisor: Yutin Huang

I study cosmological correlators as probes of inflationary physics. The bootstrap approach uses de Sitter symmetry, locality, unitarity, and analytic structure to constrain observables in the early universe—often without relying on a conventional bulk calculation.

Particle Creation from Analogue Black Holes

Advisors: Pisin Chen and Robert M. Wald

I developed a hybrid analytic–numerical framework for calculating particle creation from the moving mirror as an analog black hole.

  • Developed the Inertial Replacement Method with controlled error estimates for perfectly and imperfectly reflecting mirrors.
  • Validated the method against analytically solvable trajectories and applied it to a PIC-based Chen–Mourou plasma-mirror trajectory.
  • Found that the radiation spectrum is more sensitive to the ray-tracing mapping in the finite accelerating region than to its asymptotic behavior.

Large-Scale Structure and Baryonic Feedback

Advisor: Kentaro Nagamine

I used the cosmological simulation, CROCODILE, to study how baryonic physics modifies large-scale structure observables.

  • Analyzed matter power spectra, clustering correlation functions, and the box-size and resolution effects of the simulation.
  • Compared the contributions of different mass components—dark matter, gas, stars, and black holes—on small scales.
  • Examined the redshift dependence of baryonic feedback on structure formation.
