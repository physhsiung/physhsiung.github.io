About

Physics from first principles.

My current research focuses on the cosmological bootstrap and the analytic structure of correlators in the early universe. More broadly, I am interested in quantum field theory, gravity, and the ways that symmetry can turn difficult dynamical questions into tractable constraints.

My previous work developed a controlled analytic–numerical method for particle creation in realistic analogue black-hole systems.

Research at a glance
